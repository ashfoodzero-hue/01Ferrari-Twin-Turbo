## 4. Phase 1: the candle atlas (`docs/SWING_CANDLE_SPEC.md`; results `docs/SWING_CANDLE_ATLAS.md`; complete tables `docs/swing_book/phase1/`)

### 4.1 Rules (pre-registered, shown, approved)

- **Window:** 2013-07-14 → 2026-10-09 06:00 UTC. TRAIN = the first 60 % (→ 2021-06-23), OOS = the last 40 %.
- **Swings:** a ZigZag at 3 × ATR(14) of each timeframe. Robustness at 2× and 5×.
- **Controls:**
  - C1 failed extremes: the running extreme of its leg, with a pullback of ≥ 1 ATR, later exceeded;
  - C2 random candles: same NY hour, same year, 3 per swing, ≥ 4 candles from any swing.
- **Measures:** candles −3 … +3 around the extreme:
  - direction;
  - size / body / wicks / close location (TRAIN-C1 terciles);
  - inside, outside, engulfing, sweep-reclaim, gap, hammer / shooting star / marubozu;
  - leg context;
  - same-time context: high-impact USD release in the candle / window, surprise direction, DXY direction, silver direction, news (2020+), real volume heavy / light (2024-10+).
- **FINDING:** OOS Holm per timeframe × side, the same sign in TRAIN, vs random and at 2× / 5×. Fewer than 30 OOS swings → described only.

### 4.2 Dated changes

**Clarifications (before any number):**
- volume heavy ≥ 1.5× / light ≤ 0.67× of the 20-candle median;
- sub-period cells use their own 60/40 split;
- the FF `better` meaning;
- the candle span definition.

**ADDENDUM A, selection bias (found by auditing a 1D + 4H TRIAL run).**
- In the trial, all 10 4H "findings" sat after the extreme:
  - lows: candle +2 bearish 26 % vs 48 %, +2 bullish 64 vs 44, +1 closes high 51 vs 32, +3 bullish 59 vs 39, +1 closes low 17 vs 35, +2 closes low 19 vs 35, +3 bearish 33 vs 50;
  - highs: +2 bullish 30 vs 51, +2 bearish 58 vs 38, +3 bearish 52 vs 35.
- **Cause:** a real swing extreme is never undercut afterwards, while a failed extreme gets broken, often by the next candle. Comparing post-extreme candles partly restated the definition.
- **Fix:** at candle +k, a failed extreme counts only while still unbroken at that close. After the fix, 4H kept 2 findings (candle +1 closes in its top / bottom third at lows). **The trial numbers are discarded.**

**Performance fix:** the leg-context search was quadratic on 1m. A binary-search version gave **identical** results (992 / 992 rows) and finished in 8 min.

**ADDENDUM B, effect sizes (presentation only):** tiny < 5 pt, moderate 5-10, clear ≥ 10. On 1m-5m (9-51 k OOS swings) even 1-3 point gaps pass Holm.

### 4.4 What a real swing looks like (proven; 1H examples, real swing / failed extreme / random)

**Knowable at the close of the extreme candle:**
- The extreme candle is LARGE (top third of range):
  - lows **41 % / 29 % / 15 %**;
  - highs **37 / 23 / 19** (1m-2H).
- The SMALL-candle mirror: lows 33 / 41 / 67 (15m).
- A HIGH-impact USD release in the extreme candle or the 3 before:
  - **25 % / 16-17 % / 17-18 %**, both sides, 1m-1H;
  - inside the high candle itself: 11 / 6 / 6.
- Moderate / tiny, at the extreme:
  - outside bar: highs 25 / 18 / 14, lows 20 / 15 / 12;
  - bearish engulfing at highs 10 / 5 / 6; bullish engulfing at lows 9 / 6 / 5;
  - close location (lows close in their top third 47 / 40 / 37 at 15m).

### 4.5 What does NOT separate real swings from failed ones (measured)

- **DXY direction and silver direction** at the extreme: no meaningful difference (1m only, 1-3 points).
- **USD surprise direction** (good or bad for the dollar): tiny. Only the PRESENCE of a big release matters.
- **Real CME volume (2024-10+):** heavy volume at 3m / 5m lows 62 % vs 56 %. Tiny vs failed, but huge vs random (23 %); see §6.
- **Hammer / shooting star / long wicks / sweep-reclaim at the extreme:** tiny or moderate, or only on 1m / 3m.
- **4H and higher:** 4H 2, 6H 5, 9H and 1D 0 findings. 2D / 3D / 1W / 1M: fewer than 30 OOS swings, described only.

### 4.6 What Phase 1 means

- The extreme candle alone does not tell a real swing from a failed one.
- What separates them is the next 1-3 candles HOLDING the extreme with strong closes in the reversal direction, more often near a big USD release.
- That is early: only 7-21 % of swings are confirmed by then.
- Phase 3 (the flip ladder) measures the timing across timeframes.

## 5. Phase 2: the footprint at swing points (`docs/SWING_FOOTPRINT_SPEC.md`; results `docs/SWING_FOOTPRINT_ATLAS.md`; complete tables `docs/swing_book/phase2/`)

### 5.1 Rules (pre-registered, shown, approved 2026-10-17: "Yes, run it as written")

- **Question:** does the order flow inside the swing candles separate real swings from failed extremes, and earlier than the candle shape?
- **Data:** Databento CME gold footprints (exchange aggressor side), 2024-10-01 → 2026-10-08, joined to the spot candles by candle OPEN time. Roll candles are excluded.
- **Events:** EXACTLY Phase 1's (the swings come from the full 13-year spot series, so they are identical), restricted to the window.
- **Split:** TRAIN → OOS from 2025-12-04.
- **Measures, at offsets −3 … +3 (addendum A applies):**
  - delta (fraction terciles + sign);
  - POC location;
  - volume share in the bottom / top 10 % of the range;
  - aggressive selling at the low / buying at the high;
  - stacked imbalances (≥ 3 levels, 3 : 1);
  - absorption (≥ 15 % seller volume at the low + close in the upper half, and the mirror);
  - relative volume vs the 20 previous candles;
  - summed delta of −3 … 0;
  - delta divergence (a new low vs −3 … −1 with positive delta, and the mirror).
- **FINDING:** Holm over footprint measures only. Candle-shape measures of the same events are counted as a REFERENCE (head-to-head), never in the family.
- **Smoke:** `smoke_swing_footprint` 5/5 (planted absorption found 100 % vs 0 %; roll candles skipped).

### 5.5 What Phase 2 means

1. **The order flow AT the extreme behaves like the candle shape at the extreme.**
   - Delta divergence, heavy volume and selling into the low are 3-4× more common than at random candles, and about equally common at failed extremes.
   - They mark an extreme being made, not one that will hold (see observation 3).
2. **After the extreme, real swings show the reversal in the order flow too:**
   - high relative volume and stacked imbalances in the new direction;
   - positive delta after lows;
   - failed extremes show LOW volume on those candles.
   - But the plain candle direction / close at the same offset separates better (gaps of 15-26 points vs 10-20).
3. **Two years of footprints:** 1H+ has too few swings to prove anything (0 findings on 1H+ except one).
4. **Limitation, recorded:** the absorption definition (≥ 15 % of the candle's volume as selling in the bottom 10 % of its range) is almost never met on 15m+ (0 %). A looser definition would need a new pre-registered addendum.

## 5B. Phase 3: the flip ladder, how Bear becomes Bull (`docs/SWING_LADDER_SPEC.md`; results `docs/SWING_LADDER_ATLAS.md`; complete tables `docs/swing_book/phase3/`)

### 5B.1 Rules (pre-registered, shown, approved 2026-10-17: "Yes, run it as written")

**Anchor swings:** 3× ATR ZigZag swings on 15m, 1H, 4H and 1D, 2013-07 → 2026-10. TRAIN → OOS from 2021-06-23.

**"Timeframe X flipped at the same extreme":**
- X confirmed its own swing at EXACTLY the same price, in an overlapping candle;
- the flip time is the close of X's confirming candle;
- X ranges over all 16 timeframes.

**Measures:**
- **A. The ladder on real swings:** the share flipped, the delay, flipped before the anchor confirmed, and the order of neighbouring timeframes.
- **B. Evidence:**
  - consider every anchor extreme (real or failed) that X flipped at before the anchor's outcome was known;
  - P(real | X flipped) vs the base rate, with the lead before the anchor's confirmation;
  - Holm, Wilson lower bound > base, same direction in TRAIN;
  - fewer than 30 OOS → described only.
- **C. DXY (opposite swing) and silver (same swing):** within ±3 anchor candles, the matching share, the lead / lag, and the same at failed gold extremes.

**Smoke:** `smoke_swing_ladder` 2/2.
- Synthetic exact matches pass.
- On real data, 1H swings self-match 100 % at their own confirmation, and 15m flips at the same extreme for 99 % of 1H swings, always first.

### 5B.5 What Phase 3 means: how Bear becomes Bull

1. **The turn climbs the ladder from small to large timeframes, in order** (95-99 % up to 2H). 1m / 3m / 5m turn within minutes of the low; the anchor takes hours to confirm.
2. **The flip of the timeframe ONE STEP BELOW is real evidence.** When it confirms at the very same extreme, the chance that the anchor swing is real rises:
   - from about 40-49 % to 60-83 %;
   - hours (1H / 4H) or days (1D) before the anchor confirms.
3. **There is a trade-off between earlier and surer.** On a 4H low:
   - 1H flipped → 57 %, 22 h early;
   - 2H → 72 %, 14 h early;
   - 3H → 83 %, 4 h early.
4. **The very small timeframes (1m / 3m) flip at everything**, so on their own they mean nothing.
5. **DXY and silver turn WITH gold, not before it.** They agree more at real swings, but that can only be seen afterwards.


## 5C. Phase 3B: the next candle combined with the ladder (`docs/SWING_COMBINE_SPEC.md`; results `docs/SWING_COMBINE_ATLAS.md`; complete tables `docs/swing_book/phase3b/`)

### 5C.1 Rules (pre-registered, shown, approved 2026-10-17: "Yes, run it as written")

- **Same events as Phase 3.** Anchors 15m / 1H / 4H / 1D, real swings + failed extremes, 2013-07 → 2026-10, OOS from 2021-06-23.
- **Signs:**
  - LADDER: the timeframe one step below (5m / 30m / 3H / 9H) or two steps below (3m / 15m / 2H / 6H) confirmed at the exact same extreme;
  - STRONG +1: the next anchor candle closes in the top third (lows) / bottom third (highs) of its own range;
  - DIRECTIONAL +1: the next candle is bullish (lows) / bearish (highs).
- **No hindsight:** a combination is evaluated at T\* = the latest of its signs' known times. A candidate counts only if its outcome was still undecided at T\* (a real swing not yet confirmed; a failed extreme not yet broken).
- **FINDING:** OOS precision above the ladder alone, a Holm-passing test vs [ladder AND NOT sign], Wilson lower bound > the ladder's precision, TRAIN the same direction, n ≥ 30.
- **Smoke:** `smoke_swing_combine` 3/3, including CONSISTENCY: the ladder-alone cell reproduces Phase 3's evidence EXACTLY (every anchor, side and period).

### 5C.3 What Phase 3B means

1. **The next candle adds nothing once the ladder has flipped.** The one-step-below confirmation already contains the information of the next anchor candle: a 3× ATR move on the lower timeframe mostly IS the strong next candle. Combined: +0-3 points, not significant.
2. **A WEAK next candle barely hurts** (−2-7 points; 1D highs −11, n 21). If the lower timeframe has flipped, the swing is likely real even when the next candle closes weak.
3. **The strong next candle ALONE is a weak sign** (46-58 % vs the ladder's 60-83 %). Phase 1's "+1 closes strong" is real but much weaker than the ladder.
4. **Two steps below + strong is an earlier, weaker variant:** 4H lows 75 % (TRAIN 68 %) vs 83 % for the ladder alone. It sits between the two-step ladder alone (72 %) and the one-step ladder.
5. **Method note:** median leads come from different pools (waiting for a later sign drops the fast confirmations), so they are not comparable across cells.


## 6. Observations log (small details, kept even when untested)

1. **Swings are fractal in ATR units.** The median swing is 5.1-5.7 ATR and confirms 6-7 candles after the extreme on EVERY timeframe from 1m to 1W. The same "shape" repeats at every scale. (Phase 1.)
2. **Gold's swing highs confirm faster than its lows on the big timeframes.** Already confirmed by candle +1:
   - 2D: highs 19 % vs lows 2 %;
   - 3D: 21 % vs 3 %;
   - 1W: 18 % vs 0 %;
   - 1D: 10 % vs 3 %.

   Tops reverse sharply and bottoms grind. This may be the 2013-26 up-drift. Described only (n small). (Phase 1.)
3. **Classic "reversal signs" are signs of an EXTREME, not of a REAL reversal.** Real vs failed vs random:
   - sweep-and-reclaim at the low: 49 % / 41 % / 19 % (1m);
   - heavy real volume at the low: 62 / 56 / 23 (3m);
   - a long lower wick: 33 / 26 / 16 (1m);
   - a hammer: 24 / 17 / 10 (1m).

   Each is 2-3× more common than at a random candle, but only slightly more common than at a failed low that later broke. **They tell you an extreme is being made, not that it will hold.** (Phase 1.)
4. **Big USD releases cluster near real swings:** 25 % vs 16 % near failed extremes. **The direction of the surprise does not matter** (tiny). The news makes the turn more likely, not its direction. (Phase 1.)
5. **1m behaves differently over time:** 17 / 23 of its OOS-passing rows flip sign between TRAIN and OOS ("inconsistent"), against 0-3 on 3m and up. This fits the 2021-25 thinning of Polygon's spot feed and the general change in microstructure. Prefer 3m+ for candle reads. (Phase 1.)
6. **Random candles are SMALL** (a small extreme candle: random 67 % vs failed 41 % vs real 33 %). Any extreme, real or failed, is a bigger-than-normal candle. Always compare against failed extremes, never only against "normal" candles. (Phase 1.)
7. **DXY and silver direction AT the extreme carry no information** (1-3 points). Whether they lead earlier is a Phase 3 question. (Phase 1.)
8. **2025-04-18 → 21 (Good Friday + Easter Monday 2025) is missing in Polygon spot**, while other years' Easter Mondays exist. Flagged as a hole. (Data.)
9. **Polygon's live feed can publish minutes it later removes** (EURUSD 2026-10-13 00:00-00:28). (Data.)
10. **Polygon spot outages on 2026-10-13 22:14-16 and 2026-10-15 23:39-49 hit every symbol at once**: a provider-side outage, not ours. (Data.)

11. **Delta divergence at an extreme is everywhere** (real swing 39-51 %, failed 38-48 %, random 10-16 %). Like sweeps and hammers, it marks an EXTREME, not a REAL turn. (Phase 2.)
12. **Volume tells the follow-through.** Two candles after a real high, relative volume is HIGH 36 % vs 22 % for failed highs (15m); failed extremes' next candles are often LOW-volume (43 % vs 27 %). A real reversal leg is traded with conviction; a fake one is not. Still, the candle direction separates better. (Phase 2.)
13. **Selling into a low is normal at every low.** Only 25-29 % of lows (real or failed) have positive summed delta over −3 … 0, vs 48 % of random candles. (Phase 2.)
14. **Absorption as defined is almost never met above 5m** (0 % on 15m+). This is a definition limit, not proof that absorption doesn't exist. (Phase 2.)

15. **The ladder is orderly.** Each timeframe confirms the same low before the next larger one (95-99 % up to 2H), and only about half of 1H swings ever become 2H swings (52 %), a quarter 4H (25 %), 4 % 1D. Most swings stay small. (Phase 3.)
16. **"One step below" is the sweet spot.** 30m for 1H, 3H for 4H, 5m for 15m, 9H for 1D give 60-83 % real vs a 36-49 % base. Two steps below is weaker but earlier. 1m / 3m carry nothing. (Phase 3.)
17. **The same numbers in both periods.** TRAIN and OOS precision agree within about 5 points on every anchor (e.g. 4H lows with 3H flipped: TRAIN 82 %, OOS 83 %). The ladder looks like a structural property of price, not an era effect. (Phase 3.)
18. **DXY does not lead gold at swings.** Its opposite turn is earlier only 17-35 % of the time (consistent with the earlier Turbo-2 finding "DXY co-moves, never leads"). (Phase 3.)
19. **Silver agrees with real gold swings far more than with failed ones** (53-63 % vs 14-23 %), but only in hindsight (±3 candles). Whether a CONFIRMED silver swing before gold's confirmation adds evidence is not tested yet (open question). (Phase 3.)
20. **Base rates:** of all anchor extremes with at least a 1 ATR pullback, about 40-49 % become real swings. Any "edge" must beat this, not 50 %. (Phase 3.)

21. **Once the lower timeframe has flipped, the next anchor candle tells you nothing more** (+0-3 points). The lower-timeframe 3× ATR confirmation largely IS that strong candle seen from below. (Phase 3B.)
22. **A weak next candle does not cancel a ladder flip** (−2-7 points). Don't abandon a one-step-below flip because the next anchor candle closed weak. (Phase 3B.)
23. **Selection effect on leads:** waiting for an extra sign removes the swings that confirm fastest, so the surviving cases show LONGER median leads. Never compare medians across differently filtered pools. (Phase 3B, method.)
